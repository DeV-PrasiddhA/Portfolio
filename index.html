I’m Prasiddha Mainali, an AI builder and student researcher from Kathmandu, Nepal.

My work connects deep learning, computer vision, and practical engineering. I care about understanding systems from first principles—and turning that understanding into something useful.

From representing Nepal at IOAI to building intelligent robotics and helping students find their next opportunity, I’m drawn to problems where technology can make a tangible difference.

My computer science journey at St. Xavier’s College, Maitighar, is rooted in independent learning, experimentation, and sharing what I learn.
